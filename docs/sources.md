# Sources

The programme uses the following authoritative sources. They guide the programme design; they do not establish Northbridge-specific facts or legal obligations.

1. National Institute of Standards and Technology. (2024). *Building a Cybersecurity and Privacy Learning Program* (NIST SP 800-50 Rev. 1). [Official publication page](https://www.nist.gov/publications/building-cybersecurity-and-privacy-learning-program) · [PDF](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/SpecialPublications/NIST.SP.800-50r1.pdf)
2. National Institute of Standards and Technology. (2024). *The NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0*. [Official publication page](https://www.nist.gov/publications/nist-cybersecurity-framework-csf-20)
3. NIST Cybersecurity Framework. (2026). *Awareness and Training implementation examples*. [Official CSRC resource](https://csrc.nist.gov/projects/cybersecurity-framework/filters)
4. International Organization for Standardization. (2022). *ISO/IEC 27002:2022: Information security, cybersecurity and privacy protection — Information security controls*. [Official ISO page](https://www.iso.org/standard/75652.html)
